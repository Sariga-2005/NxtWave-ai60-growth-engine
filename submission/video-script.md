# AI60 Growth Engine — 3-Minute Video Demo Script
**Challenge:** 500 registrations with a ₹2,000 (simulated) budget in 7 days  
**Target duration:** 3:00

> Say only what the product actually does. All numbers are targets or assumptions unless read live from the app.

---

### [0:00 – 0:20] THE PROBLEM & CONSTRAINTS
* **Visual:** Title slide: 500 students | ₹2,000 budget | 7 days (targets).
* **Voiceover:** *"The goal is to get 500 final-year engineering students to register for a free 60-minute AI workshop in 7 days on a ₹2,000 budget. Paid ads alone won't reach that, so the product is built around referrals and community distribution."*

### [0:20 – 0:50] THE STUDENT INSIGHT
* **Visual:** Landing page and quiz.
* **Voiceover:** *"Final-year students want a working project, not more theory. The promise is simple: a free, 60-minute live build. A 5-question quiz recommends a project track, then leads into registration."*

### [0:50 – 1:15] THE GROWTH STRATEGY
* **Visual:** Strategy view in the app.
* **Voiceover:** *"Channels in priority order: peer referrals, campus clubs and ambassadors, WhatsApp groups, organic social, and a small paid test. The model behind these channels uses assumptions that we replace with measured data as the campaign runs."*

### [1:15 – 2:30] LIVE PRODUCT WALKTHROUGH
* **Visual:** Registration → success modal → WhatsApp share → student dashboard → workshop companion → project submission → Growth OS.
* **Voiceover:** *"A student registers and instantly gets a personal referral link with a one-click WhatsApp share. The dashboard shows their real referral count. The workshop companion is a 60-minute build checklist, and after the session students submit their project repo; automated evaluation is not available yet. In Growth OS, operators see registrations, funnel counts, channel breakdown and ambassadors from tracked data, and can draft messages — nothing is sent."*

### [2:30 – 2:50] WHY THIS CAN WORK
* **Visual:** Growth OS overview.
* **Voiceover:** *"It targets existing student clusters — clubs, hostels, WhatsApp groups — and makes sharing a single tap. Whether it reaches 500 will be measured by the Growth OS, not assumed."*

### [2:50 – 3:00] WHAT'S NEXT
* **Voiceover:** *"Next: a real Growth Copilot, Message Studio, a budget simulator, tracked club links and GitHub-based project evaluation. Thank you!"*
