# AI60 Growth Engine — Growth Plan: Path to 500 Registrations

**Campaign objective (target):** 500 final-year engineering students registered  
**Budget (simulated):** ₹2,000 | **Duration:** 7 days | **Offer:** "Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes" (free live workshop)

> All numbers in this plan are **targets or assumptions**, not measured results. Real numbers come from the Growth OS once the campaign runs.

---

## 1. Student persona & friction

* **Cohort:** Final-year engineering undergraduates (CS, IT, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, others).
* **State of mind:** Placement pressure, intimidated by AI theory, tired of long tutorial playlists.
* **Desired outcome:** A concrete AI project they can show on GitHub / resume.

| Friction | How the workshop addresses it |
| :--- | :--- |
| Tutorial fatigue | A 60-minute time-boxed live build |
| Setup barrier | Setup instructions shared before the session |
| No proof of work | Project submission (automated evaluation planned) |

## 2. Channel strategy (priority order)

With ₹2,000, paid ads cannot be the main driver. The plan relies on:

1. **Peer referrals** — every registrant gets a personal link (`?ref=CODE`) and a WhatsApp share.
2. **Campus clubs & ambassadors** — tracked via ambassador codes (tracked club links are a later task).
3. **Department / placement WhatsApp groups.**
4. **Organic social (LinkedIn / Instagram).**
5. **Small paid test** — only to learn, capped by budget.

## 3. Growth model (illustrative — assumptions only)

Registrations ≈ Σ (channel reach × conversion rate) + referral registrations.

Reach, conversion rates and the referral multiplier are **assumptions to be replaced with measured data**. The planned Simulator will let operators tune them against the 500 target and ₹2,000 budget. No projected total is claimed here.

## 4. What the product does today

1. 5-question quiz that recommends a project **track** (no fit score).
2. Registration with UTM / referral / ambassador attribution and duplicate prevention.
3. Referral link generation and one-click WhatsApp share.
4. Student dashboard with real referral counts and a referral leaderboard.
5. Workshop companion: 60-minute build checklist (not a live classroom).
6. Project submission (evaluation not yet available).
7. Growth OS: registrations, funnel counts, channel breakdown, ambassadors, experiments, CSV export, message drafts (nothing sent).

## 5. Experiments & judgment

* **Planned experiments:** headline clarity; referral CTA wording. *No results exist yet.*
* **Human judgment:** AI suggestions (e.g. budget allocation) are reviewed by a human before acting.

## 6. Execution timeline (plan)

* **Days 1–2:** Seed club ambassadors and WhatsApp groups.
* **Days 3–4:** Referral push to existing registrants; review pace against the 500 target.
* **Days 5–6:** Urgency messaging; spend remaining budget on the best-performing channel.
* **Day 7:** Run the live 60-minute build; ask attendees to share and refer for the next batch.

Contingency thresholds (e.g. "if behind pace by Day 4") will be set from real pace data, not fixed in advance.
