## SLIDE 1: Student Persona & Core Friction

### Target Audience
* **Cohort:** Final-year engineering undergraduates (Batch 2025/2026) across CS, IT, ECE, EEE, and Mechanical.
* **Psychological State:** Anxious about campus placements, intimidated by high-theory AI prerequisites, exhausted by 40-hour tutorial playlists.
* **Core Desired Outcome:** A concrete, demonstrable AI project on their resume and GitHub profile that they can confidently discuss with interviewers.

### The 3 Critical Frictions & AI60 Solutions
1. **Tutorial Fatigue:** Students drown in passive video watching without building anything tangible.  
   *→ Solution:* 60-minute time-boxed live sprint from blank screen to deployed app.
2. **Local Setup & Environment Barrier:** CUDA, Python venvs, and dependencies cause immediate drop-offs.  
   *→ Solution:* Cloud-based zero-install workspace with pre-configured API scaffolding.
3. **No Proof of Competence:** Certificates without code are disregarded by technical recruiters.  
   *→ Solution:* Automated 7-point AI Rubric evaluation + verifiable project showcase card.

## SLIDE 4: The Working Asset (AI60 Growth Engine)

Rather than building a static landing page, we engineered the **AI60 Growth Engine** — a full-stack growth platform connecting acquisition, qualification, viral loop, and post-workshop retention:

1. **Qualification Quiz ("Is This For Me?"):** 5 interactive questions calculating a live "Workshop Match Score" (e.g., 94% match) with tailored project recommendations, eliminating self-doubt before the form.
2. **Frictionless Registration Engine:** Indian mobile phone validation, graduation year verification, automatic UTM parameter attribution, and duplicate prevention.
3. **Viral Referral Console:** Instant unique referral code generation (`?ref=CODE`), copyable links, and pre-filled 1-click WhatsApp share buttons with viral copy.
4. **Student Hub & Milestone Tracker:** Live leaderboard, dynamic progress toward unlocked rewards (1, 3, 5, 10 friends), and an AI Project Idea Generator.
5. **Interactive Live Workshop Studio:** 60-minute milestone checklist tracking attendance, live Q&A feed, and cloud workspace downloads.
6. **Automated AI Project Evaluation Rubric:** 7-dimensional scoring (Problem Clarity, AI Usage, Functionality, UX, Originality, Technical Code, Completeness) issuing shareable LinkedIn builder badges that fuel the next workshop batch.
7. **Growth OS (Admin Console):** Real-time 500-target tracking, channel attribution, college performance breakdown, A/B testing suite, and an interactive campaign planning simulator.

## SLIDE 5: Experiments, AI Reflections & Scale Playbook

### A/B Experimentation Findings
* **Experiment 1 (Headline Clarity):** *"Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes"* (238 regs) outperformed *"Go From Zero to a Working AI App This Weekend"* (188 regs) by **+26.6% conversion**. Direct time-to-value beat vague promises.
* **Experiment 2 (Referral CTA):** *"Invite 3 Friends & Unlock Starter Code Repos"* (87 unlocks) outperformed *"Help 3 Friends Build With AI"* (62 unlocks) by **+40.3%**. Concrete developer assets outperform altruism.

### Human Judgment Over Artificial Intelligence
* **AI Recommendation:** The LLM suggested allocating ₹1,500 to paid Instagram influencers and running campus poster campaigns.
* **Human Rejection:** Rejected. ₹1,500 influencer spend in college tech produces fragmented reach with negligible conversion. Reallocated ₹0 spend to direct student chapter leaders (ACM/IEEE) where trust is peer-to-peer.

### Day-by-Day Execution & Contingency Matrix
* **Days 1–2 (Launch & Seeding):** Activate 8 club ambassadors; seed 25 WhatsApp placement groups. Target: 120 signups.
* **Days 3–4 (Referral Acceleration):** Trigger WhatsApp milestone challenge for existing signups. If pace is < 250 signups, launch the "Bonus 1-on-1 Code Review" unlock.
* **Days 5–6 (Urgency & Scarcity):** Launch the "Only 74 Seats Remaining" countdown push. Spend remaining ₹800 on retargeting quiz drop-offs.
* **Day 7 (Workshop & Post-Loop):** Deliver live 60-min build. Run instant AI evaluations. Direct all 384 attendees to post their builder badge on LinkedIn, driving pre-registrations for Batch 2.
